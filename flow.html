The app

Everything starts on Home. Start is the main button, the rest is for calmer moments.

Loading chart…

  • Every help page has an urgent help button, and Settings is always top right.
  • The app works without an account. An account adds history, patterns and the doctor summary.

Help session

Two minutes of breathing, then one question with four answers. The answer decides the next page.

Loading chart…

  • “How are you now? asked again” follows the same four answers as the first time.
  • “About the same” leads to something new to try the first time, and to the help page the second time.
  • After “You're getting through it” or the help page, the count starts again.
  • “Breathe at your own pace” has no timer and asks nothing. Its buttons are the answer.
  • After 20 minutes, a note at the top of every help page suggests contacting a doctor.

AI chat

Every message goes through four checks. The AI only answers when the message is safe to answer, and its reply is checked again before it shows.

Loading chart…

  • The chat says clearly that it's an AI. Messages are never saved and never include the email or name.
  • If the message is about the body (chest pain, can't breathe), the reply adds an emergency note and suggests grounding.
  • The risk check and the reply together get at most 10 seconds. After that the fixed calm message shows.
  • After “No, it's the panic”, a second warning sign in the same chat counts as a crisis.

Crisis pause

After a crisis message the AI stays quiet for 24 hours and the app points to people instead.

Loading chart…

  • The pause is kept on the phone and, with an account, on the server. It holds after closing the app and on every device.
  • There's no button to end it early.

Calm check

One minute of slow breathing with a fingertip on the back camera. The result is about the rhythm, not the heart rate number.

Loading chart…

  • Only the average colour of each camera frame is used. The video is never saved and never leaves the phone.
  • “Steadier” compares with your usual level from earlier checks. The first times it uses a fixed level.
  • The words are always kind. There's no score, no “low” and no “failed”.

Account and saving

Sign up with an email code, then the app keeps you logged in quietly in the background.

Loading chart…

  • A session is saved from its first real step, so it's kept even if it ends early.
  • Patterns show after 5 sessions and only state counts and averages, never causes.
  • The doctor summary is made on the phone. Nothing is sent unless you share it.
  • Delete my history removes sessions and calm checks. Delete my account removes everything.
