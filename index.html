Crisis pause

After a clear crisis message the AI chat is paused for 24 hours, on every device with the account. During that time the app points to people, not the AI.

5 screens
  1. Crisis messagee.g. “I want to end it all”. The server saves “paused until” 24 hours later.
  2. During the pauseThe chat shows the support screen instead of the AI. Start, closing the app or Back don't end it.
  3. Another crisisStarts the 24 hours again. “Yes, I need help now” on Are you safe? also starts it.
  4. AfterThe chat opens with a check-in. There's no “I'm safe” button to end it early.

Calm check

Fingertip on the back camera with the flash on. The message is about the rhythm, not the number: a low or high heart rate is shown but never changes the message.
